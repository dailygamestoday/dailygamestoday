# Contributing

Use Issues to suggest an independent browser game, report a broken play button, or correct a guide. The official game URL is especially helpful. If you developed the game, mention that so we can credit you and verify the link.

To report a play issue, tell us your device, browser, page URL, and what happened after you tried to start. Screenshots are welcome when they show the behavior. Crop out email addresses, room codes, session details, and current puzzle answers.

Maintainers check that a game link opens in a normal browser and that the listing describes the current version. We may ask the creator about screenshots or playable embeds before updating a page.
